## Added

- **Friend codes** — Everyone gets a short code. Type someone's in and you're
  friends instantly, both ways.
- **Find friends by name** — Search for people and send a request they can
  accept.
- **Friends tab** — Your people, in the bottom nav.
- **Friend profiles** — Their radar, what they've finished and rated, and the
  parties you've been in together.
- **Recommend to a friend** — Send a title from its page. It lands in their
  Recommended to me slot with your name attached, so they know who sent it.
- **Five statuses** — Want to, Watching, Done, Stopped, and Not for me, on
  every title page.
- **Would watch again** — A toggle that appears when you mark something Done
  and drops it into your Rewatch slot.
- **History on the You page** — Everything you've finished, with ratings you
  can edit in place.

## Changed

- **Library tab is gone** — Radar is home. Global search, quick want-to adds,
  and the stale and morning-after cards moved onto it.
- **Statuses drive your slots** — Marking something Watching or Done moves it
  between the right slots automatically, instead of you managing both.

## Fixed

- **Friends' titles leaking into your screens** — After friends launched, a
  friend's Recommended slot could appear on your radar and their watches could
  show up in your history. Every screen now shows only your own rows.
- **Stopped and Not for me not sticking** — Those statuses were dropped when
  the library reloaded. They stay put now.
- **Sluggish taps** — Status, rating, and note taps update instantly instead of
  re-downloading your whole library first, which got slow after a big import.
