## Added

- **Slot visibility** — Every slot is public, friends-only, or private, set per
  slot. Existing slots stayed friends-only, exactly as they were.
- **Profile visibility** — The same three settings for your whole profile, on
  the You page.
- **Descriptions and tags on slots** — Say what a slot is for and tag it, so
  people can tell "Halloween" from "Halloween".
- **Thumbs-up** — Like a slot you enjoy. Like counts are public; who's
  subscribed to you stays yours alone.
- **Save a slot to your radar** — Keep a live link to someone else's slot. It
  updates when they add to it, with a badge for what's new since you last
  looked, and shows how far through it you are.
- **Fork a slot** — Take a copy you can edit, with credit to whoever built it.
- **Follow a profile** — Keep up with someone's radar without being friends.
- **Taste match** — A single percentage on someone's profile showing how much
  your tastes overlap. Nothing about your ratings is ever exposed to work it
  out.
- **Explore → Slots** — Discover public and friends' slots per domain, with
  2x2 cover collages, who made them, like counts, tags, and popular or newest
  sorting.
- **Explore → People** — Find public profiles by name, with a Featured rail for
  curators.
- **Quest from a slot** — Start a party that picks only from one chosen slot,
  yours or one you've saved. Everything else about the night works the same.

## Changed

- **Slot pages open for anyone allowed to see them** — As the owner you get
  visibility, editing, reordering and your stats. As a visitor you get like,
  save, fork, share, and your own progress through it.
- **Saved slots on the Radar home** — A section for slots you've saved from
  other people, separate from your own.
- **Friends tab includes people you follow** — Not just mutual friends.
