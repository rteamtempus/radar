## Added

- **Filters search everything now** — Tap Chinese, $$, ★4+ or Open now in Eat
  & Do and the results come live from Google's entire index, filtered by
  Google itself. No more "no Chinese in Austin" just because nobody had
  looked before. Every live result joins the shared catalog for good.
- **Search on Enter** — Typing filters Radar's catalog instantly (free);
  pressing Enter or tapping "Search Google" runs the live search. No more
  wondering what a keystroke costs.
- **Overnight healing** — A nightly sweep keeps saved places fresh: closed
  places get flagged, moved pins get corrected, and places missing a rating
  fill in — so cards stop being a mix of detailed and bare.

## Changed

- Live results show a running count ("20+ live") — Google doesn't say how
  many exist in total, so Radar doesn't pretend to know.
- "Show 20 more from Google" stays a button in live mode — each page is a
  real Google call, so it stays deliberate instead of loading as you scroll.
