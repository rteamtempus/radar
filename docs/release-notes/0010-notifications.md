## Added

- **Notifications** — A bell on the You page with a count of what's waiting,
  and a matching dot on the Me tab so you can see it from anywhere in the app.
  Tap anything in the list to jump straight to what it's about.
- **What's new** — When a release lands, a single entry appears in your
  notifications. Open it once and you get every note you haven't read yet, not
  one tap per release.
- **Release notes archive** — Every release Radar has ever shipped, from the
  You page, including everything built before this system existed.
- **"They started it" and "they finished it"** — When a friend starts or
  finishes something you recommended to them, you hear about it, with their
  rating if they left one.
- **"Someone sent you something"** — The friend on the receiving end of a
  recommendation now gets told, instead of quietly finding it in a slot later.
- **Slot finished** — When someone who saved one of your slots finishes the
  last thing in it, you get to know.
- **New friend** — You're told when someone adds you with your friend code.

## Changed

- **Your profile has a header** — Your initial, your name, and the bell now sit
  at the top of the You page.
