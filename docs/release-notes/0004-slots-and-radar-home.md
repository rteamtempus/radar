## Added

- **Slots** — Curated queues that stay current instead of a single list that
  grows forever. Think "high movies", "date night", "rewatch".
- **Radar home** — A new home screen showing every slot at a glance. It's now
  the first tab and where the app opens.
- **Starter slots** — Everyone gets Watching now, Up next, Rewatch, and
  Recommended to me, ready to use.
- **Slots that behave like playlists** — Each slot decides what happens when
  you finish something in it: drop it, send it to the back of the queue, or
  leave it alone. Rewatch loops; the rest clear out.
- **Custom slots** — Make your own, name it, give it an emoji, choose whether
  it loops.
- **Reordering** — Move titles up and down inside a slot to set what's next.
- **Search inside a slot** — Add straight into the slot you're looking at.
