## Added

- **Explore** — A browsing screen over everything Radar knows, with filters
  that actually work: for films and shows, what's on your services, hide what
  you've seen, runtime, decade, rating floor, and genres; for restaurants,
  open-now, distance, price, rating, and cuisine. Sort by popular, rating,
  newest, closest, or A-Z.
- **Friend signals in Explore** — Coloured initials next to a title show which
  friends want it, are watching it, or loved it.
- **One-tap add from Explore** — Put anything you spot straight onto your
  radar.
- **Slot pages** — Every slot gets its own page: the full queue, the same
  search and filters as Explore, sorting, and reordering.
- **My Radar on title pages** — Tap to drop a title into any of your custom
  slots without leaving the page.

## Changed

- **New bottom nav** — Five even tabs — Radar, Explore, Quests, Friends, Me —
  with new icons and a gold active state. Parties are now "Quests" and live in
  a normal tab instead of a big centre button.
- **Full-bleed to the top** — The app runs edge to edge on the phone, with
  content and toasts clearing the notch and dynamic island properly.
- **Radar decluttered** — Search and per-slot adding moved to Explore. The home
  screen is now the domain switcher, your pulses, your queues as poster rails,
  and slot creation.
- **New app icon** — A heart inside a radar sweep, across every icon size and
  the iOS home screen.

## Fixed

- **Restaurant recommendations landing in your watch list** — A restaurant a
  friend sent you went into Recommended to me for films. Recommendations now go
  to the right domain, and anything previously misfiled was moved.
