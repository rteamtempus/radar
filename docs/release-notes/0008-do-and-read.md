## Added

- **Do domain** — Places to go: museums, galleries, zoos, amusement parks,
  mini golf. Searchable nearby with a wider radius than restaurants.
- **Read domain** — Books, with author, page count, year, rating, and a link
  out to Google Books.
- **Starter slots for both** — Want to go and Favourites for Do; Reading, Want
  to read and Favourites for Read.
- **Statuses that fit** — Want to go / Been there for places; Want to read /
  Reading / Finished for books, each with its own would-do-again toggle.

## Changed

- **Four domains in the switcher** — Watch, Eat, Do and Read now sit in a
  scrollable row on both Radar and Explore, with filters tuned per domain.
- **Recommendations know all four** — Sending a friend a book, a place, or a
  restaurant puts it in the matching slot on their radar.
