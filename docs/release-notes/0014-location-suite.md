## Added

- **Pick a city, explore it** — Eat and Do get a 📍 location chip. Tap it,
  type any city (or reuse a recent one), and searches, distances and nearby
  pulls all anchor there instead of where you're standing. "Near me" brings
  you home.
- **City guides** — With a city picked, Eat and Do show the places people
  save most there on Radar — with a map. Maps also work on any food-or-outing
  slot ("Map these 12 spots").
- **Slots can live somewhere** — Pin any slot to a city ("Tokyo eats",
  "Austin date nights"). Public pinned slots show up when anyone explores
  that city — with a "local's list" badge when the owner actually lives
  there.
- **Home city** — Set yours in You → Location (city-level only, never your
  exact position). It powers the locals badge and quick picks.
- **Find people by city** — Explore → People with a city picked shows people
  who chose to be discoverable there, best taste match first. This is
  strictly opt-in: You → Location → "Discoverable by city", off by default.
- **Trips** — An adventure can now have a destination, dates, and a privacy
  toggle: "Members only" (nobody else knows it exists — surprises stay
  surprises) or "Friends can see" (it appears in your friends' Quests tab).
  Friends with saved places near your destination get a nudge — "you have 12
  places in Austin, share your favorites?"
- **Your slots, your trip** — Planning an adventure with a destination? Slots
  you've pinned near it are offered as one-tap quests.
- **Report & block** — Every profile and slot page has a quiet report link,
  and profiles can be blocked. Blocked people vanish from your discovery;
  manage the list in You → Location.

## Changed

- **Slot privacy, said honestly** — The middle visibility tier is now called
  "Friends & quests": friends can browse it, and quest members can pick it
  when you're in a quest together. That's how it already worked — now the
  label says so, and each tier explains itself under the buttons.
- **Slot search is public-only** — Explore's Slots and People tabs only ever
  show public things now. Friends-only slots stay between you, your friends,
  and your quests.
