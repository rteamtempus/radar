## Added

- **Accounts** — Sign up and sign in with an email address and a password. Your
  session sticks around, so you only do it once per device.
- **Onboarding** — A quick three-step start: pick a display name, calibrate
  your taste, and tell us which streaming services you pay for.
- **Taste calibration** — Rate up to 24 well-known titles as Loved, Meh,
  Haven't seen, or Never would. Skippable after 12. Everything you tap teaches
  the app what to suggest.
- **Your library** — Search films and shows, add them to your want-to list,
  mark them finished, and rate them out of 10.
- **Title pages** — Poster and backdrop art, the blurb, genres, runtime, and
  where you can stream it, with your own services highlighted.
- **Parties** — Start a party, get a six-character code, and share a join link.
  Everyone who joins shows up in a live lobby.
- **Party constraints** — The host sets what kind of night it is: film or show,
  how long you've got, and whether it has to be streamable by everyone.
- **Mood check-in** — Before picks are generated, everyone says how much energy
  they have and picks up to three vibes.
- **AI picks** — The host taps generate and Radar builds a shortlist from
  everyone's want-to lists plus fresh suggestions, filters out anything that
  breaks the rules or that someone has already seen, scores what's left against
  the group's taste, mood, quality and freshness, then has Gemini reorder the
  finalists and write a one-line reason for each.
- **Swipe deck** — Swipe right for yes, left for no. Everyone can swipe at
  their own pace, and each person gets one anonymous veto to kill a title
  outright.
- **Voting** — Survivors go to a vote where everyone spreads three points
  across the finalists, with running totals.
- **The reveal** — Confetti, the winner, and a "watch on" link that prefers a
  service you actually subscribe to.
- **Morning-after pulse** — Twelve hours later, Radar asks whether you actually
  watched it, so your history stays honest.
- **Install it like an app** — Radar installs to your home screen, works
  offline for what it has already loaded, and shows a tap-to-update pill when a
  new version is out.
