## Added

- **Notes on your cards** — Jot down why a title is on your list, right on its
  page.
- **Recommended by** — A card can remember who told you about it.
- **Library filters** — Filter by runtime and genre with chips built from what
  you actually have, so you never see an empty filter.
- **Stale nudge** — A show you started but haven't touched in 60 days gets a
  gentle prompt: keep it, or take it off the radar.
- **Taste chips** — On the You page, set any genre to neutral, love, or avoid.
  These go straight into party picks, so you can steer the AI by hand instead
  of waiting for it to learn.

## Changed

- **Clearer lobby copy** — The party lobby now says out loud that people don't
  have to swipe at the same time.
