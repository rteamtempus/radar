## Added

- **Eat domain** — A Watch/Eat switch on the Radar home. The same slots,
  search, and title pages now work for restaurants too, and your choice is
  remembered.
- **Restaurant search** — Search by name or cuisine, or tap Nearby to see
  what's popular around you.
- **Restaurant pages** — An open-now badge, address, hours you can expand,
  cuisine, price level and rating, plus links to Maps, phone, and their
  website.
- **Eat starter slots** — Want to try, Go-to spots (which keeps its entries
  instead of clearing), and Recommended to me.
- **Location** — Radar can use your location to sort by what's near. If you say
  no, everything still works, just without distances.

## Changed

- **Statuses speak the right language per domain** — Restaurants use Want to
  try, Been there, and Not for me, with a Would go again toggle that feeds your
  Go-to spots.
